The problem

Every day, the same road carries two separate journeys

People drive between cities with space to spare while parcels wait for a dedicated vehicle.

Empty vehicle space goes unused

Cars leave Pune for Mumbai every hour with free boot space and empty seats that nobody ever uses.

Parcels and people travel separately

A parcel and a traveller take the same expressway at the same time, in two different vehicles.

Traditional delivery is costly and slow

Hub transfers, last-mile fuel and warehouse stops add days and a lot of cost to a 150 km trip.

How it works

Find Match

Pickup Verification

STEP 4

Secure Delivery

The receiver's OTP confirms delivery. Payment processing is not enabled in this demo.

Trust & safety

Built so strangers can hand over a parcel with confidence

Booking and handover status are recorded by the backend.

  • KYC Verified UsersGovernment ID and a selfie check before anyone can carry a parcel.
  • OTP VerificationA one-time code is needed at pickup and again at delivery.
  • Parcel PhotosPhotos taken at booking are compared at handover.
  • Ratings & ReviewsEvery delivery builds a public trust score for the traveller.
  • Payment integration plannedThe demo does not collect, hold, or release money.
